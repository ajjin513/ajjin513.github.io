3D Modeling

Greenhouse

Making climate change visible through 3D visualization.

Role
3D Artist & Designer
Year
2024
Medium
3D modeling & rendering
Theme
Climate awareness

Overview

Greenhouse is a 3D modeling project about the global impact of climate change. It imagines a series of virtual greenhouses, each representing a different climate zone, where viewers can see how ecosystems react to a warming planet, from melting polar ice to spreading deserts.

Concept

The project is meant to educate and engage. It shows how fragile these environments are and how changes in temperature and carbon dioxide could reshape them, making the case for climate action visually rather than with statistics alone.
